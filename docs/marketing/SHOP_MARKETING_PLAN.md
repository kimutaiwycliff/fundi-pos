## 1. Why this plan (research summary)

| Finding | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp is used monthly by ~79% of Kenyan internet users and is the favourite app of ~34%. | WhatsApp is where you **close** sales: orders, M-Pesa and delivery updates. |
| TikTok reaches ~77% monthly; Kenyan users spend ~52 min/day on it. The ad reach is ~18.4M adults. | TikTok is where new mums **discover** you. |
| Facebook still has the widest reach (~70%, ~23M users) and is home to mums' groups and local shopping. Instagram is smaller (~5M) but is the "boutique" look-book. | Facebook gives you reach and runs your ads. Instagram is where people check that you look like a real boutique. |
| 69% of Kenyan internet users research brands on social media before buying. | If your pages are empty or old, buyers go elsewhere. Consistency matters more than perfection. |
| ~72% of online buyers prefer to pay on delivery (close to 80% outside Nairobi/Mombasa). | The real problem is **trust**. Show proof: deliveries, reviews, your real shop and you. |
| The big baby shops (Nila Baby Shop ~1.9M TikTok followers, Tash Baby, Kiddie Kloset) all use the same formula: short videos, then "order on WhatsApp", then countrywide delivery. | The formula works. Copy it and stand out on curation, styling and service. |
| TikTok Shop is **not** officially live in Kenya. | Use TikTok for attention and WhatsApp + M-Pesa for checkout. |
| From 1 Oct 2026, Meta charges for WhatsApp **API** messages (≈KES 0.52 service, ≈KES 3.20 marketing). The **free WhatsApp Business app is unaffected**. Click-to-WhatsApp ads give a free 72-hour reply window. | Stay on the free WhatsApp Business app for now. |
| Small businesses typically spend KES 300–1,500/day on Meta ads, with clicks at roughly KES 10–50. | Start small (KES 500/day), test, and only scale the ads that bring orders. |

## 2. Strategy in one page

**Positioning (fill in and use everywhere):**
> "[Shop name] — beautiful, quality baby & kids' outfits and essentials in [area], styled with love. Delivery countrywide, pay via M-Pesa."

Choose 1–2 things you want to be known for, for example:
- **Curated and stylish** (not just cheap): matching sets, occasion wear, gift-ready.
- **The helpful expert**: hospital-bag lists, sizing advice, "what does a newborn actually need".
- **Gifting made easy**: ready-made baby shower and newborn hampers at clear price points.

**The customer journey and each channel's job:**

```
 DISCOVER            CHECK / TRUST          BUY                 COME BACK
 TikTok, FB groups → Instagram, Google  →  WhatsApp chat   →  WhatsApp broadcast,
 Reels, Ads           reviews, shop visit    M-Pesa / COD       next-size reminders,
                                             delivery           referrals, loyalty
```

**Your key customer types:**
1. **Expecting mums (6–9 months)**: hospital bag, newborn bundles, cots/carriers.
2. **New mums (0–12 months)**: size-up every few months, which is your best repeat customer.
3. **Gift buyers** (friends, aunties, colleagues): baby shower and "push present" hampers, often in a hurry, and they pay well for good packaging.
4. **Boutique / occasion buyers**: birthdays, Christmas, weddings, dedications.

**Golden rules**
- Every post, bio and ad ends with **one clear action**: "WhatsApp 07XX XXX XXX to order".
- **Show prices.** Kenyan buyers skip "DM for price".
- **Reply fast.** Most sales are won or lost in the first hour.
- Record **every** sale, online ones included, in the app with the customer's name and phone. Your customer list is your most valuable marketing asset.

## 3. Store redesign checklist

Design the shop so it photographs and films well. Every corner should be usable for content.

**Content corner (most important)**
- [ ] One plain, light-coloured wall (or a painted panel) with your logo on it, for product shots and talking videos.
- [ ] A clothing rail plus a small table for flat-lays.
- [ ] Lighting: shoot near natural window light, or add 1 ring light and 1 LED panel. Avoid yellow bulbs.
- [ ] A phone tripod with a clamp, kept in the corner so filming is always "ready".
- [ ] 2–3 props: a baby mannequin/dress form, a wooden hanger set, a basket for hampers.

**Shop floor**
- [ ] Group by **customer need**, not just category: "Newborn Essentials", "Hospital Bag", "Gift Hampers", "Occasion Wear", "0–3m / 3–6m / 6–12m / 1–2y".
- [ ] Clear, readable price tags (good for videos too).
- [ ] A "New This Week" display near the door, which gives you something to post weekly.
- [ ] A ready-made **gift hamper display** at 3 price points (e.g. KES 2,500 / 4,500 / 7,500).

**Till / counter**
- [ ] A QR code stand: "Scan to order on WhatsApp" + "Follow us on TikTok/IG".
- [ ] A "Leave us a Google review" QR card.
- [ ] Your M-Pesa till number printed clearly.
- [ ] A small sign: "We deliver countrywide 🚚".

**Outside**
- [ ] Clear signage with your shop name and social handle.
- [ ] Make sure the shop's location pin on Google Maps is accurate.

**Packaging (cheap but powerful)**
- [ ] Branded sticker + tissue paper + a thank-you card.
- [ ] The thank-you card says: "Tag us @handle 💛 | Refer a friend, both get KES 200 off | Review us on Google [QR]".
- [ ] Packaging should look good on camera, because customers will film unboxings.

Film the redesign itself (before → during → after). "We're opening our new look" is excellent launch content.

## 4. Week-by-week launch plan (Oct 5 – Nov 1)

### Week 1 (Oct 5 – 11): Foundations

**Accounts and profiles**
- [ ] Choose one handle and use it everywhere (e.g. `@shopname.ke`).
- [ ] Create or clean up: TikTok Business, Instagram Professional, Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Bio template on all platforms:
  ```
  👶 Baby & kids outfits + essentials | 🎁 Gift hampers
  📍 [Area, Town] | 🚚 Countrywide delivery
  💳 M-Pesa Till XXXXXX | 📲 WhatsApp to order 👇
  [wa.me link]
  ```
- [ ] Profile picture: your logo. Cover/banner: a nice photo of the shop.

**WhatsApp Business setup** (details in [§10](#10-whatsapp-business-playbook))
- [ ] Business profile: address, hours, description, email, website/social links.
- [ ] Greeting message, away message and quick replies.
- [ ] Labels: `New lead`, `Ordered`, `Paid`, `Delivered`, `VIP`, `Expecting`, `Gift buyer`.
- [ ] Catalog with your **top 30 products** first, organised into collections.

**Google Business Profile**
- [ ] Category: "Baby store", with secondary categories "Children's clothing store" and "Boutique".
- [ ] Hours, phone, WhatsApp, M-Pesa info, delivery area.
- [ ] 15+ photos: outside, inside, products, you/staff.
- [ ] Ask your 10 most loyal customers personally for a review this week.

**Products and photos**
- [ ] From the app: **Reports → Top products** (last 90 days). This is your "hero" list.
- [ ] Photograph the top 30 on your content wall: front, detail/fabric, and one styled/flat-lay.
- [ ] Prepare a price list per collection, including your hamper price points.

**Content bank**
- [ ] Film 10 short videos in one session (see the ideas in [§9](#9-content-playbook)), so you have a buffer before you start posting.

### Week 2 (Oct 12 – 18): Start posting every day

- [ ] Post 1 video per day on TikTok, and repost the same video to Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.
- [ ] Post 3–5 WhatsApp Status updates per day.
- [ ] Join 5–10 Kenyan mums' Facebook groups (by area: e.g. Nairobi mums, Kiambu mums, Mombasa mums). Read the rules and help people first; promote only where it's allowed.
- [ ] Respond to every comment, because it boosts reach.
- [ ] Mashujaa Day (Tue Oct 20) is next week: prepare a small offer, e.g. "Mashujaa Weekend: free delivery within [town] on orders over KES 3,000".

### Week 3 (Oct 19 – 25): Turn past customers into repeat buyers

- [ ] Export your customer list from the app (name + phone + what they bought).
- [ ] Message each customer **personally** (not as a broadcast) to ask if they want updates. Use the opt-in template in [§10](#10-whatsapp-business-playbook).
- [ ] Add those who say YES to WhatsApp broadcast lists by type: `Newborn`, `Toddler`, `Gift buyers`, `Boutique`.
  - Broadcasts only reach people who **saved your number**, so ask them to save it.
- [ ] Launch the **referral offer**: "Refer a friend, you both get KES 200 off". Track it by hand (note it on the sale) until the app supports it.
- [ ] Run the Mashujaa weekend offer and note how many orders it brings.

### Week 4 (Oct 26 – Nov 1): First ads and Black Friday prep

- [ ] Pick the 2–3 organic videos with the most views or messages.
- [ ] Run a **Click-to-WhatsApp ad** on Facebook/Instagram: KES 500/day for 7 days (setup in [§11](#11-paid-ads-playbook)).
- [ ] Optional: boost 1 TikTok video for KES 500–1,000 (TikTok Promote) to test.
- [ ] Black Friday plan (Fri **Nov 27**):
  - Which slow stock to clear (anything unsold for 60+ days)
  - 3 bundles (e.g. "Newborn Starter Pack", "Hospital Bag Set", "Christmas Outfit + Accessory")
  - Order Christmas stock **now** (occasion outfits, red/green/white sets, gift hampers)
- [ ] Book 1–2 mum influencers for November (see [§12](#12-influencers-referrals-and-reviews)).

**By Nov 1 you should have:** all profiles complete, 20+ posts live, a WhatsApp catalog of 30+ items, 10+ Google reviews, a first broadcast list, ad results, and a Black Friday plan.

## 5. Season plan (Nov 2026 – May 2027)

| Period | Theme | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Nov 2 – 22** | Black Friday build-up | Daily "countdown" teasers; "Join our WhatsApp list for early access" (this grows your list); influencer posts. |
| **Nov 23 – 30** | Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Nov 27 – 30) | Early access for broadcast customers 24h before; bundles + clearance; go live on TikTok on Friday; ad budget up to KES 1,000–1,500/day for 5 days. |
| **Dec 1 – 24** | Christmas outfits and gifts | "Baby's first Christmas" sets, family matching, gift hampers; "Order by Dec 20 for upcountry delivery"; Jamhuri Day (Dec 12) weekend offer. |
| **Dec 26 – Jan 3** | Holiday / New Year | Light posting; "New Year, new sizes" for growing babies. |
| **Jan** | Back to school / budget month | Toddler and daycare essentials, value packs, "January budget bundles", payday timing (end of month). |
| **Feb** | Valentine's | "Mum & baby" matching, gifts for new parents, "love your little one" sets. |
| **Mar – Apr** | Easter | Easter outfits; family photo shoot styling. |
| **May** | Mother's Day (2nd Sunday) | Gifts for mums, push presents, pampering hampers. Partner with a photographer or salon. |
| **All year** | Baby showers, dedications, birthdays | Always have ready-made hampers and an "occasion outfits" collection. |

**Payday rhythm:** Kenyan spending peaks around the end and start of the month (25th – 5th). Schedule offers, broadcasts and higher ad spend for those days.

## 6. Daily routine

About 60–90 minutes a day in total. A shop assistant can take over parts of it.

| Time | Task | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning (8–9am) | Post 1–2 WhatsApp Status updates (new arrivals, a "today's pick"). Reply to overnight messages. | 15 |
| Midday (12–2pm) | Post today's TikTok/Reel (from your content bank). Cross-post to IG + FB. | 15 |
| Afternoon | Reply to comments and DMs within an hour. Answer group questions where you can help. | 20 (spread out) |
| Evening (7–9pm, peak time) | 1–2 more Status updates (a delivery being packed, a customer review). Reply to messages. | 15 |
| Close of day | **Record all WhatsApp/online sales in the app** with customer name + phone + note of the channel ("TikTok", "IG", "Referral – Jane"). Update WhatsApp labels (Ordered → Paid → Delivered). | 10 |

**Daily checklist**
- [ ] 1 short video posted (TikTok + Reels + FB)
- [ ] 3–5 WhatsApp Status updates
- [ ] Every DM/comment answered
- [ ] All sales recorded in the app with customer details and channel
- [ ] Orders dispatched with a "your order is on the way" message

## 7. Weekly routine

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Plan the week: check the app's **Top products** and **Low stock / Restock suggestions**. Promote what's selling, restock it, and decide which slow item to push. |
| **Tuesday** | **Batch filming** (1–2 hours): film 7–10 videos for the coming week at your content corner. |
| **Wednesday** | Send the **one weekly broadcast** to each list (new arrivals or one offer). Never more than 1–2 per week. |
| **Thursday** | Post in 2–3 mums' Facebook groups (where allowed). Update the WhatsApp catalog with new items and remove sold-out ones. |
| **Friday / Saturday** | **TikTok LIVE** (30–60 min): try-ons, "bundle under KES 3,000", Q&A on sizing. Announce it in Status first. |
| **Sunday** | Review the numbers (see [§15](#15-what-to-measure)), ask 3–5 happy customers for Google reviews, and rest. |

**Weekly checklist**
- [ ] 7 videos posted
- [ ] 1 broadcast per list
- [ ] 1 TikTok LIVE
- [ ] Catalog updated
- [ ] 3+ new Google reviews requested
- [ ] Ads checked: pause losers, keep winners
- [ ] KPI tracker filled in

## 8. Monthly routine

- [ ] **Run one themed campaign** (see [§5](#5-season-plan-nov-2026--may-2027)).
- [ ] **"Next size" outreach:** message customers who bought newborn/0–3m items 3–4 months ago: "Baby must be growing! Our 3–6m sizes just arrived 💛". This is your best repeat-sales trigger.
- [ ] **Birthdays:** message customers whose baby turns 1 this month (if you've collected the date) with a birthday outfit offer.
- [ ] **One influencer / creator partnership** (see [§12](#12-influencers-referrals-and-reviews)).
- [ ] **Clear dead stock:** anything not sold in 60+ days goes into a bundle or a "Last pieces" sale.
- [ ] **Google Business Profile:** add 5 new photos, post 2 updates/offers, reply to every review.
- [ ] **Review the money:**
  - Sales by channel (walk-in vs WhatsApp vs referral)
  - Ad spend vs orders from ads, i.e. cost per order
  - Repeat-customer rate
  - Average order value
- [ ] **Decide next month's ad budget.** Increase only what produced orders.
- [ ] **Refresh your top 30 catalog items** with new photos if needed.

## 11. Paid ads playbook

### Ad #1: Click-to-WhatsApp (main ad)
- **Where:** Meta Ads Manager (Facebook + Instagram), objective **Engagement → Messaging → WhatsApp**.
- **Creative:** your best-performing organic video (proven content beats new content).
- **Audience:**
  - Women 20–40 within 10–15 km of the shop, plus your main delivery towns
  - Interests: parenting, baby products, pregnancy, motherhood
- **Budget:** KES 500/day × 7 days to start.
- **Text:** "Beautiful baby outfits from KES 1,500 💛 Delivery countrywide. Tap to chat and order on WhatsApp."
- **Bonus:** chats started from these ads get a **free 72-hour messaging window**.

### Ad #2: TikTok Promote (test)
- Boost a video that's already doing well organically. KES 500–1,000 test, aiming for profile visits or website (wa.me) clicks.

### Ad #3: Retargeting (once you have traffic)
- Show your offer to people who watched 50%+ of your videos or messaged you in the last 30 days. These are cheap, warm audiences.

### Rules
- Run each ad for at least 5–7 days before judging it.
- Judge by **WhatsApp chats and orders**, not likes.
- **Kill** ads where cost per order > 25% of the average order value. **Double** the ones that stay below 15%.
- Ask every new customer **"How did you hear about us?"** and record it in the app.
- Increase spend around payday (25th – 5th) and before holidays.

## 12. Influencers, referrals and reviews

### Mum influencers / micro-creators
- **Who:** Kenyan mums with **5k–30k followers** in your area, with real engagement (comments, not just likes). Micro-creators convert better and cost less than celebrities.
- **How to find them:** search TikTok/IG for `#kenyanmum`, `#nairobimum`, `#momlifekenya` plus your town. Note who your own customers follow.
- **Offer:** free outfit/hamper (KES 3,000–6,000 value) + a personal discount code (e.g. `JANE10`) + KES 200 for every sale with her code.
- **Ask for:** 1 TikTok/Reel + 3 Stories, tagging you, showing the WhatsApp number.
- **Track:** sales with her code, so you rebook only the creators who sell.

### Referral programme
- "Refer a mum: you both get KES 200 off your next order."
- Record the referrer on the sale in the app (in the notes, until we build proper support).

### Reviews
- Aim for **50+ Google reviews** in 6 months.
- Ask in person at the till (point to the QR card), on the thank-you card, and by WhatsApp 2 days after delivery.
- **Reply to every review**, positive or negative, politely.
- Screenshot great reviews and WhatsApp messages (with permission) for Status and TikTok.

## 13. Delivery, payment and trust

- **Delivery options to set and publish:**
  - In-town rider (same day)
  - Nairobi delivery
  - Upcountry via a courier or matatu parcel service
- **Payment:** M-Pesa Till number in every bio and on every receipt.
  - For new customers, especially upcountry, consider **pay on delivery** for in-town orders, or a small deposit for upcountry.
- **Trust builders:**
  - Real shop photos and location on Google Maps
  - Delivery videos
  - Customer reviews
  - A clear exchange/return policy, e.g. "Exchange within 7 days if the size doesn't fit, item unworn with tags"
- **Speed:** confirm the order → payment → dispatch with a message at each step.

## 16. Rules: consent, privacy and children's photos

- **Children in content:** only film or photograph children with **written permission from the parent/guardian**. Never show a child's full name, school or location. Mannequins, flat-lays and "hands-only" shots are safer and still work well.
- **Marketing messages:** Kenya's **Data Protection Act (2019)** requires consent for direct marketing. Only broadcast to customers who said YES, and honour "STOP" immediately.
- **Customer screenshots/reviews:** ask before sharing, and hide phone numbers and names unless permitted.
- **Platform rules:** follow each Facebook group's promotion rules, or you'll be removed.

## Packaging: a paid "Sell Online" add-on

These features are offered as an **opt-in paid add-on** that tenants pay extra for, rather than being given to everyone.

### What exists in the app today
- `Tenants.subscriptionTier` has the values `trial | starter | growth | enterprise`. Only platform admins can change it, and changes are written to the platform audit log ([Tenants.ts](../../apps/api/src/collections/Tenants.ts)).
- The public pricing page lists **Starter KES 1,000/mo (1 store)**, **Growth KES 2,800/mo (up to 5 stores)** and **Enterprise (custom)** ([page.tsx](../../apps/web/src/app/page.tsx)).
- **No code checks the tier yet.** `billingStatus` controls login/lockout ([billing.ts](../../apps/api/src/lib/billing.ts)), but `subscriptionTier` is only a label. Even the advertised store limits aren't enforced.
- Billing is **manual**: a platform admin sets the tier after payment. Pesapal and M-Pesa in the app are for the shop's own customers, not for paying for the subscription.

### Decision (agreed 5 Oct 2026): an add-on, not a new tier
**The platform admin can switch "Sell Online" on or off for each tenant at any time, from the platform panel.** It doesn't depend on the tenant's tier.
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **A. New "Pro / Commerce" tier above Growth** | Simple ladder | A **single-store boutique** (the main target, e.g. this shop) would have to pay for multi-store features it doesn't need, so it probably won't upgrade. |
| **B. Add-on on top of any paid tier** ✅ | Matches what buyers need: "I have 1 shop and want to sell online". Can be added to Starter or Growth. Easy to trial and to cancel. | One more thing to bill. |

**Suggested packaging:**

| Plan | Price / month | Sell Online add-on |
|---|---|---|
| Trial (14 days) | Free | Admin can switch it on for the trial so shops try it before paying |
| Starter | KES 1,000 | + KES 1,500 → **KES 2,500** |
| Growth | KES 2,800 | + KES 1,500 → **KES 4,300** (add-on covers all stores) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Usually included (admin switches it on) |

Prices are a starting suggestion. Validate them with 3–5 real shops. A useful check is that the add-on should cost less than one extra sale a week for a boutique.

### How it would work technically
1. **Entitlements layer** (build this first)
   - Add a platform-admin-only `addons` field on `Tenants` (e.g. a multi-select: `sell_online`), plus `addonsChangedAt`. Log changes in the existing platform audit log, the same way tier changes are logged today.
   - Add one shared helper, e.g. `hasFeature(tenant, 'storefront')`, which works out access from **tier + add-ons + billingStatus**. Rules: trial = everything; enterprise = everything; `canceled` = nothing.

2. **Enforce on the server, hide in the UI**

3. **Downgrades are safe**

4. **Platform panel:** an add-on toggle on the tenant page next to the existing tier/billing form, plus an add-on column on the tenants list.
5. **Self-serve billing later:** add-ons start out manually managed like tiers. Later, owners could pay by M-Pesa from inside the app and have the add-on switch on automatically.

## Online shop SEO — how to get found on Google

Everything below lives in **Settings → Online shop → Search engines (SEO)** (add-on on), plus the online fields on each product.

### What the app already does automatically
- **Pages:** one page per shop and per product, each with a canonical URL. The shop page's own title and description come from your SEO settings, with sensible defaults if left blank.
- **Link previews:** Open Graph and Twitter tags, so links shared on WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram show a photo, name and price.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** a Store entry (name, town, WhatsApp, catalogue) and a Product/Offer entry per product (price in KES, in stock or sold out). This helps Google show prices and availability.
- **Sitemaps:** each shop gets its own at `/shop/<your-address>/sitemap.xml`, and live shops also appear in the site-wide `sitemap.xml`.
- **Privacy:**
  - A shop that's paused, or that has "Show my shop on Google" turned off, is marked *noindex* and left out of the sitemaps.
  - Customers with the link can still order from a "hidden" shop.

### One-time setup (about 30 minutes)
1. **Shop address:** pick a short, descriptive one, e.g. `mama-baby-westlands`. Avoid changing it later, because old links and Google results would break.
2. **Search title** (≤ 60 characters), for example "Baby Clothes & Newborn Gifts in Westlands, Nairobi". Put *what you sell + where* first.
3. **Search description** (≤ 155 characters): say what you sell, from what price, delivery and how to order. Example: "Newborn sets, baby shower hampers & kids' outfits from KES 800. Countrywide delivery, M-Pesa. Order on WhatsApp."
4. **Town / area:** e.g. "Westlands, Nairobi". This feeds local searches like "baby shop near me".
5. **Share image:** upload a bright 1200×630 photo of your shop or best products.
6. **Google Search Console** (free):
   1. Go to search.google.com/search-console → **Add property** → **URL prefix**, and enter `https://app.fundipos.co.ke/shop/<your-address>/`.
   2. Choose **HTML tag**, copy it, and paste it into "Google Search Console verification". Save Settings, then click **Verify** in Search Console.
   3. In Search Console → **Sitemaps**, submit `https://app.fundipos.co.ke/shop/<your-address>/sitemap.xml`. Use the **Copy sitemap link** button in Settings.
7. **Google Business Profile:** set the profile's **Website** to your shop link. This is one of the strongest local-ranking signals.

### Per product (as you list items)
- **Online description:** write 2–3 honest sentences for people, covering fabric, sizes, who it's for and gift ideas. Don't copy a supplier's text, because Google ignores duplicated content.
- **Search title / description:** optional. Use them for your best sellers, e.g. "Newborn cotton romper set 0–3m".
- **Photos:** use real photos on a clean background. The first photo becomes the Google and WhatsApp image.
- **Category names:** use the words people search for, e.g. "Newborn", "Baby Shower Gifts", "Boys 1–3 years".

### Ongoing (ties into the weekly and monthly routine)
- **Weekly:**
  - List new arrivals online with good descriptions, because fresh pages get crawled.
  - Share product links (not just photos) in WhatsApp Status, bios and Facebook groups. Every share is a visit, and links from other sites help ranking.
- **Monthly:**
  - Check Search Console → **Performance** to see which searches show your shop. Add those words to titles and descriptions.
  - Look for products with views but no orders, and improve their photos or price.
  - Ask for Google reviews. Reviews lift your Business Profile, which links to the shop.
- **Backlinks:** put the shop link in your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook bios, your Google Business Profile, Kenyan business directories and partner shops' pages.
- **Realistic timeline:**
  - Google usually indexes a new shop within days to a few weeks.
  - Ranking for competitive searches ("baby shop Nairobi") takes months of consistent listing, reviews and sharing.
  - Specific searches ("newborn hamper Westlands") come sooner.

### Later options (not built)
- **Custom domain**, e.g. `mamababy.co.ke`. It's the biggest SEO upgrade for serious shops, and needs DNS plus per-domain routing.
- **Blog-style buying guides** on the shop, e.g. "Hospital bag checklist". These pull in searchers who aren't ready to buy yet.
